Noah Reviews is a static website. We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, ad networks or sign-up forms.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security. Product images load from our partner's image servers (Shopify), which receive standard request data when they serve an image.

When you click a partner link, the partner's own privacy policy applies on their site.
