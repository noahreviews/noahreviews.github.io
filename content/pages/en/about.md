Noah Reviews publishes buying guides and product reviews for home, holiday and everyday products. Our goal is simple: explain what actually matters before you buy, in plain English, and be upfront about the downsides.

## What we cover

We started with holiday lighting (Christmas curtain lights, animated window displays, smart lights and balcony decorating) and add new categories over time.

## How we are funded

Some brands we cover are partners, including Lumalyn. When a guide links to a partner, we say so at the top of the page and mark the link as sponsored. Details are in our [affiliate disclosure]({{page:affiliate-disclosure}}).
