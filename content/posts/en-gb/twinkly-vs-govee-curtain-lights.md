Searching for **Twinkly vs Govee** usually means you have already decided you want a smart LED curtain, the kind that shows pictures and animations rather than just glowing, and now you want to know which brand to pick. This comparison uses only the specifications each brand publishes on its own product pages, plus one calculation of our own: how many LEDs each curtain fits into a square metre. We also cover buying in the UK.

> **Disclosure:** we are affiliated with Lumalyn, which we include as a third option. Twinkly and Govee are not partners of ours, and nothing here is sponsored by them.

Govee's US page gives sizes in feet; the metric figures are our conversions.

## Pixel density: which curtain shows sharper pictures?

For a curtain that displays images, the number that matters most is how tightly the LEDs are packed. We divided each brand's published LED count by its published area:

| Curtain | LEDs | Area | LEDs per m² |
|---|---|---|---|
| Twinkly Curtain 210 | 210 | 1.5 × 2.1 m = 3.15 m² | about 67 |
| Twinkly Curtain 400 | 400 | 3 × 2 m = 6 m² | about 67 |
| Govee Curtain Lights 2 (H70B4) | 1,040 | 9.8 × 6.6 ft ≈ 6 m² | about 173 |
| Lumalyn, 2 × 2 m | 400 | 4 m² | 100 |
| Lumalyn, 3 × 3 m | 400 | 9 m² | about 44 |

Higher density means finer detail and smoother curves, especially when people look from a few metres away. From across the road, all of these curtains read as clear pictures; the difference shows most on a window or balcony seen up close.

## Weather rating and where you can hang them

Twinkly rates the Curtain **IP44**, which means splash resistant: fine for covered balconies, porches and indoor windows. Govee lists **IP65** for Curtain Lights 2, which adds protection against water jets and suits spots where the British rain hits. Either way, keep adapters dry (Govee's UK page says its UK-plug adapter is for indoor or dry locations only) and use an RCD-protected outdoor socket. Our [Christmas curtain lights guide]({{post:christmas-curtain-lights}}) explains the ratings.

## Apps, control and music

- **Twinkly** connects over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, so you can control it from anywhere on your home network. Music sync works with Twinkly Music. Twinkly also notes that several Curtains can be grouped in the app to act as one display.
- **Govee** uses the Govee Home app, supports Matter and voice assistants, offers 8 music modes, and lets you draw, upload photos or GIFs, or generate effects with its AI Lighting Bot. Govee notes that the smallest model (H70B3) cannot be spliced to other units.

## Buying Twinkly lights or Govee curtain lights in the UK

- **Twinkly** lists several plug types; choose the Type G (UK three-pin) version, not a foreign plug on a travel adaptor.
- **Govee** has a UK store with prices in pounds; model names and sizes can differ from the US page, so confirm before ordering.
- **Other Govee Christmas lights or Twinkly lights?** The same checks apply: UK plug, IP rating, UKCA or CE marking and a reputable seller.
