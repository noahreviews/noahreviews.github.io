For many families, Christmas lights are about more than Santa and snowmen. A light up nativity scene, a bright star or an angel tells everyone walking past what Christmas means to you, whether you have a front garden or just a window in a flat.

## Light up nativity scene options at a glance

| Option | Best for | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| Light-up figure sets | Front gardens, porches | Need anchoring and an outdoor socket |
| Silhouettes and outlines | Lawns, walls, fences | Light and easy to store |
| A Star of Bethlehem | Windows, small spaces | Simplest; works on its own |
| Pixel curtain showing a nativity | Flats, balconies | You draw or import the design |

## Light-up nativity figures for the garden

Moulded or wire-frame figures of Mary, Joseph and the baby Jesus are the traditional outdoor nativity scene. Wire-frame versions are lighter and easier to store but catch the wind, so stake them into the lawn or weigh the bases down.

## Nativity silhouettes and outlines

A stable roof outline in warm white, or flat silhouettes of the holy family, turns a fence, wall or patch of lawn into a scene and packs flat for storage.

## A Star of Bethlehem light

A large star above the scene or on the roof line is visible from far away. If space is tight, a single star in a front window says it all.

## UK weather and safety for nativity lights

- **Plan for rain and wind, not snow:** IP44 under a porch, IP65 or higher in the open.
- **Keep plugs and transformers indoors**, as Electrical Safety First advises.
- **Use a 30 mA RCD-protected socket**, which BS 7671 requires outdoors, and an outdoor-rated extension lead.
- **Buy UKCA or CE marked lights** from a reputable seller.
- **Set a timer.** It is dark by about 4 pm in December.

## How to create a nativity design on a pixel curtain

1. **Start with a silhouette.** A stable roof, two kneeling figures and a manger read clearly from the street.
2. **Use few colours.** Warm white, gold and one deep blue are usually enough.
3. **Add the star last.** A bright gold star above the stable is the most recognisable element.
4. **Check it from the pavement** before you save, and fix anything that looks blurred.
5. **Save a calm and a festive version**: a still scene for late evening, a twinkling star for early evening.

## Faith message ideas

Short text reads best from the street: Joy to the World, Silent Night, O Holy Night, Emmanuel or Peace on Earth.

## Keep it respectful

- Place the nativity at eye level or slightly raised, never on the ground among inflatables.
- Light it in soft warm white or gold, away from flashing novelty lights.
- In Britain, decorations traditionally come down at Twelfth Night, the eve of Epiphany (6 January). Some families keep the crib up until Candlemas on 2 February.
