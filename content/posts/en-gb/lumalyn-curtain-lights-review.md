## What it is, and what it isn't

Ordinary curtain lights have hundreds of LEDs that all do the same thing at once: glow, twinkle or fade. A pixel curtain like Lumalyn's gives every LED its own address, so the controller can light them in patterns that form pictures and moving animations. That's the difference between "a balcony with some lights on it" and "the balcony the whole street points at".

It isn't a projector, and it isn't a string for the guttering. It's built for one surface: a railing, a window or a set of patio doors, seen from the street or from inside the room.

## Picture quality: what 400 LEDs can show

With pixel lights, sharpness depends on how tightly the LEDs are packed. Both Lumalyn sizes use 400 LEDs, so the size you pick also sets the density:

| Set | Area | LEDs per m² | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 × 2 m | 4 m² | 100 | Sharper shapes and text; best for a window or a short railing |
| 3 × 3 m | 9 m² | about 44 | Bigger picture, softer edges; best seen from the street |

Bold shapes read best: a tree, a snowman, Father Christmas, stars, hearts, short words. From across the road, both sizes show clear pictures. Up close, the smaller set looks noticeably crisper. If you want photo-like detail at arm's length, a denser curtain (see the comparison below) will suit you better.

## Designs, app and remote

This is where the curtain earns its keep. Out of the box you get **100+ animated scenes**: Christmas classics, snowy scenes, festive messages and colour patterns, plus other seasonal themes, so the lights needn't go back in the loft on Twelfth Night. In the app you can:

- switch scenes whenever you like, or let them cycle;
- **draw your own pixel design** or type a message that scrolls across the curtain;
- change brightness, speed and colour, from 16 million options.

The app connects over **Bluetooth**, so there's no Wi-Fi password, router or hub to set up. The trade-off is range: you control the lights when your phone is nearby, not from the office. The included remote handles the everyday switching, and the **timer** turns the display on at dusk and off at bedtime without you lifting a finger.

## Music sync

Five music modes make the curtain pulse and change with whatever is playing, which is the bit that turns a balcony into a small party. It isn't the same as a fully choreographed light show, where every beat is programmed by hand on a computer. If you want that level, read our guide to [syncing Christmas lights to music]({{post:christmas-lights-to-music}}). For most homes, five modes are plenty, and they work in seconds.

## Power and safety

The curtain runs on **5 V USB power**. That has two practical benefits:

- **Extra-low voltage** is the safest kind of Christmas lighting to have near people, pets and metal railings.
- **No outdoor socket needed.** Plug the controller into any USB phone charger indoors and run the cable through the door seal, or use a power bank for a few hours of display.

The one thing to know before you order: **a USB charger isn't included.** Any phone charger with a standard UK three-pin plug will do the job on 230 V mains, and most homes have a spare in a drawer, but check before the lights arrive. If you plug into an outdoor socket instead, make sure it's protected by an RCD (residual current device) and keep the charger dry.

## Weather rating: where you can hang it

Lumalyn rates the curtain **IP44**, which means splash resistant. That covers indoor windows, covered balconies, porches and patio doors under an overhang. It isn't designed for open guttering or a railing that takes the full force of the rain. If your spot is fully exposed, look for IP65 or higher. Govee, for example, lists IP65.

## Installation

Hanging it takes minutes and no tools:

1. Fix the hooks along the top of the railing or window frame.
2. Hang the top wire on the hooks; the strands simply drop down.
3. Connect the controller, plug it into USB power, and pair the app.

There's no drilling, which makes it a good fit for renters. For railings, windows and walls, our guide to [hanging curtain lights without drilling]({{post:how-to-hang-curtain-lights}}) has step-by-step methods.

## Who should buy it, and who should skip it

**Buy it** if you have a balcony railing, a street-facing window or a covered porch and you want a display that changes every night without losing a weekend to setup. It's especially good for flats, where you can't drill or run new wiring, and for families who enjoy picking the scene of the day. If you'd like faith-based scenes, Lumalyn also has a [Christian edition page]({{store#christian}}) with nativity, star and angel designs.

**Skip it** if your lights will hang in the open rain, if you want to control them while you're away from home, or if you need the finest possible pixel detail for close viewing.
